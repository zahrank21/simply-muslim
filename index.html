Simply Muslim

Privacy Policy · Effective November 6, 2026

In short: Simply Muslim has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your location, prayer tracking, bookmarks and settings stay on your devices. We don't collect, sell or share personal information.

What stays on your device

The app saves these only on your iPhone (and your Apple Watch, if you use it). We never receive them:

  • Your location, used to work out prayer times, the Qibla direction and nearby mosques.
  • Your settings, such as calculation method, notification choices and daily recitation time.
  • Your prayer, fasting and Ramadan reading ticks, tasbih counts, bookmarks and where you stopped reading.

Deleting the app removes this information. If you use the Apple Watch app, your location and settings are copied between your iPhone and your watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity.

Location

The app asks for your location while you are using it. It is used on your device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. It is not sent to us, and it is not sent to the services listed below. When you search for nearby mosques, the search is made through Apple Maps and is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Camera

The camera is used only for the augmented reality Qibla view, to show the direction in front of you. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Notifications

Prayer and daily recitation notifications are scheduled on your device. No notification service of ours is involved.

Services the app connects to

To show content, the app downloads it from these services. Like any website or app that downloads something, these services can see your device's IP address and which item you asked for (for example, a surah or a page of the Quran). We don't send them your name, your location or any identifier.

  • Quran.com / Quran Foundation – Quran text, translations, recitations and page fonts. quran.foundation
  • AlAdhan.com / Islamic Network – adhan recordings. aladhan.com

Their own privacy policies apply to what they collect.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you use Siri or Shortcuts with the app, your requests are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The app only answers with prayer times or opens the Quran.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools in the app.
  • We never sell or share personal information.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
